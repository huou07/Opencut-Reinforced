# Review gates

Run these before rendering and again before saving. Each gate is a question, not
a checkbox you can tick by habit.

## 1. Story gate (per beat)

* If this beat were cut, would the viewer lose information, understanding or a
  memorable moment? If not, rewrite or delete it.
* Does the beat answer "what happens, why, and what comes out of it" — not just
  "what exists"?
* Is the previous beat's idea different from this one in *viewpoint* (user view,
  close-up, inside, comparison), not only in colour?

## 2. Sound-off gate

* Mute the audio. Can you still tell what is happening? A beat where only text
  appears and disappears fails this gate.
* Does the narration name a thing that is actually on screen at that moment?
  Generic animation under specific words is a fail.

## 3. Sync gate

* Does each caption appear at or after the voice reaches that phrase? Nothing may
  lead the read.
* Do captions sit in the reserved strip and never cover the illustration?
* Are cue lines short enough to stay on one line (≤ ~46 characters)?

## 4. Narration gate

* Does any line claim a feature or number that was not verified in the repo docs
  or the running app?
* Are paid services described as paid? Only free/local things may be called free.
* Is every technical term explained at the moment it is needed, in plain words?

## 5. Assembly gate

* Do the tracks end together (narration ≈ music ≈ video)?
* Are there zero gaps and zero overlaps on the video track?
* Does every marker sit on a beat boundary?
* Did you save explicitly, and does `project summary` show the new revision?

## 6. Honesty gate (reporting)

* Say which steps were verified in this session and which were not.
* Never claim the narration was listened to, or the video watched, if it wasn't.
* Never claim a harness or provider is supported unless the build's code and docs
  show it.
