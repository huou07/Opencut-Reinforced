# Edit

## Rhythm

- Build the cut on the narration's beat grid, not on a music loop.
- Cut two frames before the word ends; the next image arrives on the syllable.
- One cut per beat minimum; a static shot longer than four seconds needs an
  internal move.
- No crossfades between beats. A hard cut is the style; a transition never runs
  longer than 200 ms.

## Gaps and holds

- 250 ms between spoken lines; let the last frame of a beat hold through it.
- Hold the final frame 300 ms after the last word before the music ends.
- Never cut mid-word and never leave a black frame between beats.

## Sound

- Music sits at −9 dB under speech; duck it, do not lower the whole mix.
- Music starts at frame zero and fades out over the last second.
- No sound effect on every cut; one accent per act at most.

## Check

Watch the first, middle and last ten seconds. If any cut lands on a breath or a
black frame, move it to the nearest word boundary.
