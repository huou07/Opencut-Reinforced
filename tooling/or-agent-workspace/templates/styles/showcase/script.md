# Script

## Budget

- 17–20 characters per second of Vietnamese narration at 180 wpm.
- One sentence per beat, 6–12 words.
- `duration_ms ≈ characters / 18 × 1000 + 250`; round up, never down.
- Speech covers 45–60 % of the beat; the rest is the image breathing.

## Shape

- Subject, verb, object. Concrete verbs: open, cut, render, speak.
- Numbers as they are spoken; no parentheses, no slash forms, no acronym soup.
- One name per thing; do not alternate between synonyms.
- The narration is what is said; the caption is the same meaning shortened to
  fit two lines. They may differ, but never contradict.

## Test

Read the line aloud once. If you stumble, the viewer will too; rewrite it
shorter. If a sentence needs a comma to survive, split it into two beats.

## Forbidden

Filler openings ("in today's world"), stacked adjectives, claims with no visible
proof, and sentences that describe the interface instead of the result.
