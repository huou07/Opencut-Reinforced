# Captions

## Style

- 26 pt, anchored bottom, offset −420 px at 1080p.
- One style for the whole film: same size, anchor and offset on every cue.
- Two lines maximum; about 42 characters per line.
- Captions are clips on a caption track, never burned into a render.

## Timing

- 12–18 characters per second of reading speed.
- A cue lives 0.8–7 s. Split at punctuation, never mid-phrase.
- Start on the word, end a few frames after it; do not pad every cue to the
  same length.
- A cue never overlaps the next one.

## Wording

- Keep the speaker's words; fix only obvious recognition errors.
- Keep diacritics. Shorten the line instead of stripping marks.
- No speaker labels, no sound descriptions, no decorative punctuation.

## Check

Read the first, middle and last cue on the timeline. If a cue is clipped or
unreadable at normal speed, split it and re-import the SRT.
