# Story

## Arc

Seven beats, 55–75 s total:

1. **Hook** (3–5 s) — the outcome the viewer gets, stated as a fact.
2. **Problem** — the friction the product removes, shown in one image.
3. **Deconstruct** — the product opened up: the pieces the viewer will see.
4. **Core action** — the main editing or creation act, performed on screen.
5. **Assist** — the part a person could not do alone, with the result visible.
6. **Craft** — motion, voice or finish that proves quality, not a feature list.
7. **Close** — identity and one next action.

## Rules

- One idea per beat. If a beat needs two headlines, it is two beats.
- Every spoken claim has a visible proof in the same beat.
- The hook is not a logo, a title card or a greeting.
- The close is not a feature recap; it is the product name and one action.
- Speech covers 45–60 % of the runtime. Silence is punctuation, not a gap to
  fill.
- Beat order is fixed; beat length follows the narration, not the reverse.
